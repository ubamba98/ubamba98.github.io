I am a Member of Technical Staff at [Unconventional AI](https://unconv.ai/), working toward making AI inference 1,000× more efficient. My research focuses on efficient language models and inference, including flow-based parallel generation, mixture-of-experts models, and model compression.

Previously, I was an Applied Scientist II at Amazon Lab126, where I worked on efficient GenAI for Alexa+ through dense-to-MoE conversion, quantization-friendly training, and compression for on-device and cloud inference. Before that, I built multimodal models and efficient ML workflows at Amazon Ads, researched resource-efficient machine learning at Mila, and co-founded Transmute AI Research at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.

I am also a [Kaggle Competitions Master](https://www.kaggle.com/ubamba98) (top 0.02%, peak rank 45 worldwide). Outside work, I enjoy gaming, cycling, and badminton. For updates, see my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PgnnH78AAAAJ) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ubamba98/).
